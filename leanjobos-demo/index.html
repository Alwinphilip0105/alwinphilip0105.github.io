LeanJobOS · walkthrough

A job search that fills the forms for you and waits for your yes.

LeanJobOS finds roles that fit, prepares each application, and fills it in Chrome on your laptop while you watch from your phone. Nothing is sent without you.

Personal project · in active development Fills in your laptop's Chrome, 3 at a time LinkedIn + Indeed each morning, free US only · entry to 3 years

Screens are from my own running copy, captured 28 September 2026. Company names and counts from my real job search are blurred; "Acme Robotics" is a stand-in.

How it fits together

Three parts: the server, your laptop, your phone

The server runs all the time and holds everything. A small program on each laptop — the worker — is the only part that touches application forms. It asks the server for work, opens a Chrome tab per application and reports every step back, so you can follow along anywhere.

The screens

A tour of the app

Today

What needs you, then what's next

  • An application that needs you is one line with Open — the details live on its own page.
  • Your next step: drafts ready for review, or new matches.
  • Applying now, with Watch & chat, Stop and Stop all.
/runs/<id> — the live application

Watch it fill, answer it, talk to it

  • What it needs from you is at the top: answer the question, tap Ready, or say I submitted it.
  • The laptop's screen, every two seconds.
  • What it's doing step by step, and a chat box: "put 120000 for salary". Tick Remember for this site to keep it.
  • The Résumé tab shows the exact file being attached.
Jobs

Your matches, newest first

  • Jobs found in the last day are marked New; each has a match score and the skills you have or lack.
  • Filter by Where (a city, a state, Remote), pay, visa stance, kind of work.
  • Prepare application to review and send, or Fill in Chrome to have it filled and submit it yourself.
  • Paste any job link at the top — LinkedIn and Indeed jobs are looked up on the company's own site.
Applications

Everything you sent, and what came back

  • Every application with its status, when it went and the proof.
  • Employer emails move them along on their own: acknowledged, interview, offer, rejected.
Insights

Where the jobs are

  • Open jobs by state: strong matches, median posted pay, H-1B filings, postings that rule out a visa.
  • Which companies have the most openings in your fields.
Settings → Passwords & site notes

Sign-ins it uses, notes it follows

  • Sign-ins for Workday, Randstad, Kforce and any site, encrypted on the server.
  • For Workday it makes the account itself and saves the password here; you only click the email link.
  • Notes per site that every application there follows. (Sign-ins shown are examples.)
On the phone

The same app in your pocket

Add it to your home screen. A notification tells you when an application needs you; tapping it opens that application's live page.
